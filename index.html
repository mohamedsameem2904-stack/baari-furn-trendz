Our Approach

Furniture, considered from every angle

Luxury 360° isn't a slogan we picked for the sign — it's how we walk a customer through a purchase. Every sofa, cot, dining table and mattress on our floor is shown, touched and sat in before it's chosen, with our team talking through fabric, wood and fit for your specific room.
